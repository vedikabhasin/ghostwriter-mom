8th Wall is open source. Now what?

On February 28, 2026, 8th Wall's hosted platform shut down. The engine is now MIT-licensed, but SLAM, VPS, Maps and hand tracking did not come with it. Every guide to what comes next is written by a vendor.

What you actually lost

- World tracking (SLAM)
- Visual positioning (VPS)
- Hand tracking

The shortlist

Zapworks, Kivicube, Blippar and Arloopa each cover part of it. Read the announcement.
